Project Name

PromptPlanner

Vision

PromptPlanner is a personal finance management platform designed to help individuals and families track income, expenses, bills, loans, EMIs, lending, borrowing, and upcoming payments in a simple and secure way.

Problem Statement

Many individuals struggle to manage:

Daily expenses
Monthly bills
Credit card payments
Loan EMIs
Borrowing and lending records
Reminder tracking

Financial information is often scattered across notebooks, spreadsheets, SMS messages, and multiple applications.

Solution

PromptPlanner provides a single platform where users can:

Record income
Track expenses
Manage recurring bills
Track EMIs
Manage lending and borrowing
Receive payment reminders
View graphical reports
Target Users
Students
Employees
Families
Small business owners
Freelancers
